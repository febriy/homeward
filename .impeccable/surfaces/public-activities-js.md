# Homeward practices

Mode: Operate for choosing a personal plan; Read for short how-to instructions.

## THESIS
Move from understanding an idea to trying one small action in everyday family life. A saved plan and a reported attempt are different states.

## OWN-WORLD
Extend the incumbent paper, forest, Georgia and native-control learning dashboard. Preserve its lesson cards and one-task player. No new identity, rasters or mascot.

## STORY
Lessons / Practices navigation → choose meal prep or dinner check-in → short concrete instructions → choose/edit one action → choose/edit a cue → saved ready-to-use plan → try it away from the screen → record what happened and an optional note → repeat or adapt later. No reminders are implied.

## FIRST VIEWPORT
Two equal practice cards with concrete outcomes and one action each. A ready plan surfaces its cue and Open my plan. The player uses the familiar narrow column. Planning asks one question per screen; the ready state shows When / I’ll prominently and keeps the how-to accessible.

## FORM
Precisely specified extension to the existing learning UI; inherit its structure under new-work.md's local-extension exception. Three-step instructions are deliberately concrete. Suggestions are editable starting points, never automatic commitments. Explicit Next and Save & leave persist drafts; unsaved state is visible. Earn 20 XP once for the first self-reported try of each practice, including an imperfect attempt; planning and Not yet earn none. Histories preserve the action, cue and reflection. No claims of habit mastery, streaks or fixed habit timelines.
