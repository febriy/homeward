# Homeward micro-lessons

Mode: Read, followed by Operate for the personal reflection.

## THESIS
A lesson earns its place by teaching one small idea before asking anything of the reader. Read, notice yourself, save, close. Awareness is a complete outcome.

## OWN-WORLD
Preserve Homeward's paper, forest green, Georgia headings, platform body type, native controls and quiet workbook rhythm. These are short pages from a practical home workbook, not a course dashboard or achievement game.

## STORY
Choose a real lesson. Read concise authored teaching with an everyday example. Reflect on three or four statements with an explicitly anchored 1–10 scale or Not sure. Optionally name one change. Save a dated private reflection; close without an obligation. Revisit older reflections or take a fresh snapshot later.

## FIRST VIEWPORT
The library names the learning outcome of each available lesson in open typographic rows. A lesson opens directly on its actual teaching, with its title and a short reading estimate. No XP, prerequisite, task checklist, level or account gate. Signature interaction: the reader moves from the idea to their own separately recorded ratings, with no aggregate judgement.
